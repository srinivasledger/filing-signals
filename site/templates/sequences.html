Sequences

Companies that have reached more than one signal. A raw filing feed shows each event alone; the order they arrive in is often the more informative thing.

A recognisable progression runs late filing → auditor change → going concern → non-reliance. Not every company follows it, and reaching one step does not imply the next. Dates are filing dates.
